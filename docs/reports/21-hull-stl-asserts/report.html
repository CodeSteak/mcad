2 Phase A: Offset, hull, minkowski

Kernel offset (spike)

Kernel::offset(s, d, tag) is the Minkowski sum of a body with a sphere: facade sc_offset via SmApiOffsetBrepFull (outward, rounding, self-intersection handling). The spike (state 83ee5b3, volume from the mass properties against the Steiner formula) shows where SMLib is exact: convex edges become cylinders and corners become spheres, as long as the faces meet at right angles at the corners. On the tetrahedron the surfaces are correct (4 planes, 6 cylinders, 4 spheres with exact centers and radii), but the trim curves lie up to 0.33 mm off the edges (volume +4.5·10⁻⁴ vs. Steiner); on the 45° wedge SMLib fails. The facade therefore checks the distance of the result boundary to the body (9 points per edge, face center) and the trim curves against the 3D edges (10⁻⁶ of the size), and otherwise reports "offset is not exact".

module platte(w = 60, t = 30, h = 4, r = 5) {
    difference() {
        linear_extrude(h) hull() {
            for (x = [0, w], y = [0, t]) translate([x, y]) circle(r);
        }
        for (y = [t / 4, 3 * t / 4]) translate([w / 4, y, -1]) linear_extrude(h + 2) langloch(w / 2, 5);
    }
}

Deviations: 2D hulls and prisms ≤ 10⁻⁸ (integration); solids of revolution (capsule, spheres, dome) 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁶ – accuracy of the volume integration of surfaces of revolution, the geometry itself is exact (STEP: SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION, no B-splines); cube + "point": the point is a cube with 10⁻⁴ edge length (+2.9·10⁻⁶ is its contribution).

Limitations

  • Offset (and thus minkowski with a sphere) only on bodies whose faces meet at right angles at the corners; tetrahedra, wedges, oblique polyhedra → warning, children unchanged.
  • Equal spheres with centers in one plane (rounded plate) are not a special case (the hull of the centers is flat, an offset of it does not work) – case "4 equal spheres in one plane" above; workaround: linear_extrude of a 2D hull with $fillet.
  • rotate_extrude children, torus and $fillet primitives in hull: warning.

Up to 500 faces, import + one Boolean stays at 1.3 s (445 faces). Above that the Boolean time grows superlinearly (890 faces 2.8 s, 4,646 faces 86 s); the times scatter between neighboring sizes because the number of intersection edges at the poles depends on the subdivision of the sphere. Realistic exports lie just above the limit (more faces than a sphere with the same triangle count because of the bores) and automatically become meshes; with as = "solid" build and one Boolean take 1–1.5 s.

  • Point contact (sphere on plate) is detected, but reported and drawn without location.

D1 Kernel errors with location

If a Boolean result is not a closed solid, the facade (version 6) returns the non-manifold edges as polylines; the engine knows the node per operand and names the touching operands. JSON diagnostic with at (context coordinates) and nodes; mcad render marks every location (edges red, point, label „Berührung Z.15 + 16“ = contact lines 15 + 16), the UI does the same, and a click on the message frames the location.

Equal spheres with centers in one planenot a special case (flat hull of the centers); warning.
